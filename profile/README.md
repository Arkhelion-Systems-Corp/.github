# Arkhelion

**Your life, connected.** The intelligence that's on your side.

One private record of your life, on your own Mac, under a key only you hold. There is no server of mine holding it, so there is nothing on my side to leak, to sell, or to hand over.

It starts with health, for people with chronic, complex or unexplained symptoms who have seen many doctors and still have no clarity.

**What connects today:** body (Oura, Apple Health), calendar (Apple Calendar, ICS import), notes (Notion, written history), work (Gmail, Drive, Google Calendar).

**What does not:** money and relationships are named in the architecture and are not built. There is no iPhone app yet. Answers still go to a cloud model through your own key. The app is not signed or notarized yet. All of that is on the site in plain words rather than glossed over, because a claim you can check is worth more than one you cannot.

**Public download: September 29, 2026.** macOS first, iPhone next.
